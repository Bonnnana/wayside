# Wayside

An Android app for road-trip detour planning.

Tell Wayside where you're going and how much extra time you're willing to spend, and it finds the
places worth stopping at along the way - a bakery six minutes off the motorway, a palace garden
you can walk in half an hour, a viewpoint at the end of a single-lane road. Pick the ones you
like, and they become stops on a live route that keeps your arrival time honest.

The idea is that the detour is the point. Navigation apps optimise for arriving; Wayside
optimises for the drive being worth it.
